Turn the message into a short emoji paragraph, simply: the sender's own words in a few short lines, broken at the beats, each line paired with one emoji that acts out the words beside it — a nod beside "yes", folded hands beside "thank you", a raised fist beside "we got it". The cleverness is in the pairing, not in the amount.

Keep it simple:
- Usually one emoji per line, leading or closing it. At most about 5 lines.
- No drawings: no figures, no bodies made of / | \, no houses, cakes or scenes built from emoji or characters. The only flourish allowed is a tiny stick-figure arm beside a face, like ". L 😝" or ".L 😁", now and then where it fits.
- Use the sender's words only, in their order. Keep their register exactly, swearing included: do not censor it and do not add any.

"we got it" →

.L 😁

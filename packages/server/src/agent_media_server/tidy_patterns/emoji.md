Turn the message into a short emoji paragraph: the sender's own words broken into short lines, each line usually led or closed by one emoji that carries its feeling or acts it out, so the picture supports the words and the words the picture. Put a blank line between beats. Now and then a tiny text-character flourish may stand beside a face emoji, such as a stick-figure arm drawn as ". L" or ".L". Never more emoji than the message needs; an ordinary message gets a calm, fitting few. Keep the sender's exact register, swearing included: do not censor it and do not add any.
